NovaTech Solutions - IT Security Policy (Version 2.0)

1. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and a special character. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, and all cloud consoles. The approved authenticator app is Microsoft Authenticator.

3. Laptops and Devices
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Personal USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours by emailing it-helpdesk@novatech.example or calling extension 4357.

4. Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer data is always Restricted and must never be shared on public AI tools or personal email.

5. Phishing
Suspicious emails must be reported using the Report Phishing button in Outlook. Employees must complete phishing awareness training every 6 months.
