NovaTech Solutions - Leave Policy (Version 3.2)

1. Annual Leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is credited at the rate of 2 days per month. A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Annual leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Paternity leave is 15 working days of paid leave, to be used within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

4. Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Employees in their probation period of 6 months are not eligible for work from home, except in emergencies approved by HR.

5. Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published on the HR portal every December.
