# NovaTech CloudDesk - Product FAQ

## What is CloudDesk?
CloudDesk is NovaTech's cloud-based helpdesk software for small and medium businesses. It offers ticket management, a knowledge base, live chat, and AI-powered reply suggestions.

## What plans are available?
There are three plans. Starter costs USD 15 per agent per month and includes ticketing and email support. Growth costs USD 35 per agent per month and adds live chat, automation rules, and reports. Enterprise costs USD 70 per agent per month and adds AI reply suggestions, SSO, and a dedicated success manager.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes, every plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial.

## What is the refund policy?
Annual subscriptions can be refunded on a pro-rata basis within the first 30 days. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable but can be cancelled at any time.

## What are the support hours?
Starter and Growth customers get support Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST. Enterprise customers get 24x7 support with a 1-hour response time for critical issues.
