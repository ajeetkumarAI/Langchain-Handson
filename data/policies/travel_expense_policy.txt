NovaTech Solutions - Travel and Expense Policy (Version 1.5)

1. Booking
All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days in advance. Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours for employees at Senior Manager level and above.

2. Hotel Limits
The maximum hotel allowance is INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night in non-metro cities.

3. Daily Allowance
The daily food allowance is INR 1,500 for domestic travel and USD 60 for international travel.

4. Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from the trip, with original receipts uploaded to the expense portal. Claims submitted after 30 days require approval from the department head. Reimbursement is processed within 10 working days of approval.

5. Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses above INR 10,000 require prior approval from the business unit head.
